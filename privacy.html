Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

KidsSongStudio is a personal tool. Only its owner uses it, to manage their own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, KidsSongStudio gets permission to upload and manage videos and playlists on the owner's YouTube channel, and to read channel and video information. It does not request the owner's contacts, email or files.

How the data is stored

  • The OAuth tokens that Google issues are stored only on the owner's computer, in a file only the owner can read.
  • Video metadata and statistics are used only on the owner's computer, to publish and track the owner's own videos.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Viewers and children

KidsSongStudio collects no information from the people who watch the videos, including children. All videos are marked "made for kids" on YouTube. YouTube's handling of viewer data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

To revoke KidsSongStudio's access, go to Google Account permissions. You can also delete the token file on the owner's computer. Deleting the file erases all stored credentials.

Third-party terms

By using KidsSongStudio you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
